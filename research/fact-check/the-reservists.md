# Fact-check: the-reservists

- Document checked: content/research/en/the-reservists.md (researchedAsOf 2026-10-07)
- Checked by: Stage 3 agent (not the Stage 2 author) on 2026-10-07
- Claims checked: 168 · Corrected: 22 · Removed: 2 · Sources replaced: 2 (source 66 replaced; 1 source added as 67)
- Limited-information flag: false (the party has a detailed published platform and a 10-point governance plan, it answered four Ynet issue questionnaires in September–October 2026, its leader has a full Knesset and ministerial record from 2019–2022, and coverage is wide; the gaps are no Knesset voting record of its own, no combined joint-list platform, and no position on marriage, conversion or Shabbat)

Every one of the 66 original sources was opened. News and party pages were downloaded and read as raw text; i24NEWS [45] was read through its public content API (api.i24news.tv/v2/he/contents/artc-7a1a5882) because the page itself is rendered by JavaScript. The Times of Israel [3, 31] was read with WebFetch. The CEC candidate list [10] is an image-only PDF; it was rendered and read visually. The Knesset OData service [23, 32] returned HTTP 474 (access denied) to both curl and WebFetch throughout this session. Those two sources were checked against the same Knesset tables (KNS_PersonToPosition, KNS_BillInitiator, KNS_Bill, KNS_Status) as mirrored by Open Knesset at production.oknesset.org (files dated 6 Oct 2026). The official URLs were kept. The document cites no Knesset plenum votes, so no vote tallies needed paginating. The only government vote it cites, Hendel's 2022 abstention on the Lebanon maritime deal, was checked against [56, 57].

## Notes for the consistency review
- **Tiering of Ynet questionnaire answers [50, 58, 59, 62]:** the answers are attributed in each article to the joint list ("המילואימניקים-הכלכלית" / "מהרשימה המשותפת של יועז הנדל וירון זליכה נמסר"). They are kept as **Formal commitment**, the same call the Yisrael Beytenu fact-check made for its Ynet questionnaire answer. If the corpus decides these are Statements, four paragraphs here need re-tagging.
- **The August 2026 "Shield of David" plan [43–45]** was issued by Zionist Home–The Reservists with Tropper, who has since left. It is tagged Formal commitment and attributed "with former partner Chili Tropper" each time. JPost [49] notes that voters "are no longer voting for the same political framework". Reviewers may want to treat it as weaker evidence.
- **Knesset OData was unreachable** (HTTP 474) for the whole session. Sources [23] and [32] were checked through the Open Knesset mirror; the official URLs were left in place.
- **Gaps that remain:** there is no joint-list platform; Zelekha's own economic positions are covered in the NEP file. The coalition section relies mostly on Hendel's interviews (Statements), as is normal for a party with no record in government.
