## Legal section verification
- The statement that no indictments, convictions or official inquiries were found was checked with a Hebrew search for State Comptroller findings, investigations and indictments involving Lapid. Two results came up:
  - The State Comptroller's October 7 examination. Lapid met its team on 5 Sep 2024, and the Comptroller said the audit covers the political, civilian and military levels (Arutz Sheva report of the meeting, [87]). This is an official act, so it was added as a fact. No published Comptroller finding concerning Lapid was found.
  - A January 2019 private criminal complaint that was referred to the State Prosecution. No indictment or official finding followed in any source found. It is an allegation, not an official finding, so it was not added.
- Nothing official was found on Ben-Ari, Tibon, Cohen or Ben Barak. Bennett's matters are covered in byachad-bennett.md.
