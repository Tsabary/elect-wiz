# Fact-check: eretz-yisrael-shelanu

- Document checked: content/research/en/eretz-yisrael-shelanu.md (researchedAsOf 2026-10-07)
- Checked by: Stage 3 agent (not the Stage 2 author) on 2026-10-07
- Claims checked: 164 · Corrected: 31 · Removed: 4 · Sources replaced: 0 (7 sources added, [62]–[68]; the list is numbered 1–68 with no gaps, and every source is cited)
- Limited-information flag: false (The party itself has no platform, website or campaign and only two candidates, but it is not a party with little reliable information. It was a separately funded Knesset faction in the 25th Knesset, audited by the State Comptroller. Both of its candidates have long documented records in this Knesset: Wasserlauf as a minister since late 2022 and Kroizer as an MK since January 2023, faction chair, Judicial Selection Committee member and committee chair, with legislation, statements and wide coverage. This matches the registry decisions so far, which set false for parties built around people with a substantial record even when the party has no platform of its own. The case differs from haredi-public, where the people behind the party had no comparable record. The `#information-availability` section was rewritten so that it no longer starts with "**Limited information.**". It still explains plainly that the evidence is about the two candidates, not the party as an organisation.)

## Legal section verification
- **State Comptroller, party accounts (Feb 2025):** verified in the official report "דוח על תוצאות ביקורת החשבונות השוטפים של הסיעות בכנסת העשרים וחמש לתקופה שבין 1.12.22 ל-31.12.23" (catalogue 2025-P-001, שבט התשפ"ה / February 2025) [3]: 16 factions funded, including "ארץ ישראל שלנו - מפלגה יהודית מאוחדת לשלמות התורה העם והארץ"; section 12(a) treats each party in a joint faction as a separate faction for current funding; Table 1 lists "א"י שלנו" among four factions that were not given a positive report, for activity contrary to the Election Period Payments Taxation Law 1996 (failure to report and pay on time), with NIS 20,000 of funding withheld; the text says "א"י שלנו" paid about NIS 222,000 of special tax late. Kept: it is a State Comptroller finding about the party. No later reversal was found.
- **State Comptroller, Negev governance (Apr 2026):** verified in the official summary "היבטי משילות בנגב - ביקורת מעקב מורחבת (תקציר)", אייר התשפ"ו / April 2026 [58]: the Ministerial Committee for the Development of the Negev, the Galilee and National Resilience, chaired by the minister (Government Decision 274), had not convened at all in the two years since its creation, to the end of the audit in February 2025, and the Comptroller recommends that the minister convene it. Kept in #legal as a State Comptroller finding about a body Wasserlauf chairs, worded so that it is not implied to be an offence, and noting his January–March 2025 absence from office [17, 18]. Also kept in #other-negev-galilee.
- **High Court, 30 May 2024:** verified in ICE [39] (petition by Minister Wasserlauf on selective enforcement in protests rejected; his X post quoted). Removed from #legal (petitioner, no finding against him), with a one-line explanatory note; kept in #issue-judicial-system.
- **Searches on 7 Oct 2026** for Wasserlauf or Kroizer with investigation, indictment, police, Attorney General and Knesset Ethics Committee found no official proceeding against either. Ben Gvir's matters belong to the partner party's document.

## Joint-list and split-history verification
- **CEC filing (IDI-hosted copy, published 07.09.2026, updated 09.09.2026) [1]:** the list "עוצמה יהודית" was "submitted on behalf of the Jewish National Front party and on behalf of the Eretz Yisrael Shelanu – a united Jewish party for the wholeness of the Torah, the people and the land party". Only two candidates are marked "מטעם מפלגת ארץ ישראל שלנו": No. 3 "וסרלאוף יצחק שמעון" and No. 6 "קרויזר יצחק". Nos. 1–2, 4–5 and 7 onward are filed for the Jewish National Front. Matches `content/registry/lists.json` (list `otzma-yehudit`, memberPartyIds `jewish-national-front` + `eretz-yisrael-shelanu`, ballot letters ב), `parties.json` (leader recorded as Wasserlauf, top-ranked candidate) and `research/ground-truth.md` §3 row 14.
- **Ballot letter ב:** Ynet's report of the CEC's 27.09.2026 publication [4] lists "ב – עוצמה יהודית בראשות איתמר בן גביר".
- **Disqualification:** JPost (4 Oct 2026) [5]: CEC vote 19–10 with one abstention on 24 Sept; the High Court rejected The Democrats' appeal on procedural grounds and the individual requests (not signed by one-third of the committee). Matches ground-truth.
- **Leader:** no source names a formal chair. Ground-truth's "top-ranked candidate" rule (Wasserlauf) stands; IDI (2022) says EYS is "represented by" Wasserlauf [63] and Ynet says it is registered "in the names of" Wasserlauf and Kroizer [2].
- **Split history:** July 2022: Knesset House Committee approved Otzma's technical split from the RZ faction in the 24th Knesset, and Wasserlauf was replaced as deputy faction representative (JDN, 3 July 2022) [9]. 20 Nov 2022: Arranging Committee approved the three-way split "as agreed between them when they merged" (Walla) [8]; IDI confirms that JNF and EYS were the two parties representing Otzma in that split [63]. Jan 2025: Otzma left the coalition and returned on 19 Mar 2025 [17, 18] (a coalition exit, not a faction split). No split between JNF and EYS was found.
- **Point 1 (shelf party and funding):** Ynet, 7 Sept 2026, 17:34 UTC, by Amir Ettinger [2]: "בן גביר רשם עוד 'מפלגת מדף' בתוך רשימתו על שמם של השר יצחק וסרלאוף וח"כ יצחק קרויזר, ששמה 'ארץ ישראל שלנו', כדי לקבל עוד יחידת מימון בשווי שנתי של 1.83 מיליון שקל", "כפי שעשה בבחירות 2022". Figures and wording confirmed. The Comptroller report [3] confirms separate funding in the 25th Knesset.

## Notes for the consistency review
- **limitedInfo = false** is a judgement call. The party as an organisation is a funding vehicle with no platform, which is the case for true. I set false because its two candidates, and the party itself as a funded faction, have a full 25th Knesset record, and false is consistent with the partner jewish-national-front and with other leader-based entries. The information-availability text still states plainly that the evidence concerns the two candidates.
- **October 7 inquiry:** the closest option is now explicitly a weak inference from government and coalition decisions. No statement by either candidate was found. Reviewers may prefer "No documented position found" for consistency with how other parties without candidate statements are handled.
- **Gaza 5 Oct 2025 item:** relabelled from Change over time to Statement (unnamed sources are not an official position). Check other Otzma-related documents (jewish-national-front, national-union-tkuma) for consistent handling of the same Ynet report.
- **Wasserlauf's MK status:** not independently confirmed for July 2023 or today. Kroizer replaced Eliyahu, not Wasserlauf, under the Norwegian law [64], and the JNF log's per-MK records show Wasserlauf voting in 2024–2026, which suggests he kept his seat. The document therefore avoids saying he is "currently an MK".
- **The 2008 founding** (Marzel and Wolpo) is reported by Ynetnews [62]. No source shows that today's registered party with the long name is the same legal entity, so the text says only that a party of this name was announced then.
- Several sources are right-leaning or sectoral outlets (Srugim, JDN, Arutz Sheva, Kipa, Kikar HaShabbat). They are used for the candidates' own words or for events that are corroborated (for example, vote tallies by Kan and Walla). Kroizer's claim in [65] about Haredi parties and an importer is attributed to him and to unnamed sources.
