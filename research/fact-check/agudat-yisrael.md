# Fact-check: agudat-yisrael

- Document checked: content/research/en/agudat-yisrael.md (researchedAsOf 2026-10-06)
- Checked by: Stage 3 agent (not the Stage 2 author) on 2026-10-07
- Claims checked: 168 · Corrected: 37 · Removed: 5 · Sources replaced: 2 (Wikipedia [20] → IDI party page; Times of Israel op-ed [35] → Globes news report), plus 7 sources added ([78]–[84])
- Limited-information flag: false (the party has a long Knesset and government record from 2022 to 2025, a signed coalition agreement, official Knesset position records and heavy Hebrew news coverage; the gaps are no general platform, no per-MK votes for the 25th Knesset in open data, and no documented position on Iran or the October 7 inquiry). The orchestrator should set `limitedInfo: false` and `researchedAsOf: 2026-10-06` in the registry. #information-availability does not start with "**Limited information.**".

Method: every cited URL was opened. Hebrew news pages (JDN, INN, Kol Rega, Haredim10, Srugim, Mako, Israel Hayom, Globes, Maariv) and the PDFs (CEC list copy, IDI explainer, State Comptroller report) were downloaded with curl and read in full. The coalition agreement [32] is a scanned PDF, so its pages were rendered and read as images (clauses 8, 21–36, 60–63, 82–132 checked). Times of Israel, Ynet, i24, JC and Globes (English) were read with WebFetch. Knesset OData (KNS_PersonToPosition, KNS_Person, KNS_Faction) was queried directly. The Knesset Votes OData service returned no records for the 25th Knesset, which confirms the document's note that per-MK votes are not available there.

## Legal section verification
- **State Comptroller annual report 59B (2009), "ענייני מינהל ושכר ברשתות גני ילדים במגזר החרדי"** [31]: opened the official PDF on mevaker.gov.il and read the summary and the Beit Yaakov chapter. Confirmed: audit March–September 2008; 80% of the network's kindergartens funded by the ministry and municipality; teachers' pay cut by 10% or more; assistants paid for 11 months or less despite funding for summer months; "מינהל לא תקין של הנהלת בית יעקב, בזבוז כספי ציבור ושלילת זכויות שכר"; management "must pay immediately". Goldknopf is not named in the extracted text. The link to Goldknopf rests on his own statements [56, 27] and on Israel Hayom's reporting [82] that the "Beit Yaakov" Jerusalem network was managed by him until the 2022 election.
- **Tel Aviv Regional Labor Court, class-action settlement approved April 8, 2024** [82]: reputable reporting of an official court act (Israel Hayom, 2024-04-09). Confirmed: approval date ("yesterday, Monday"), ~NIS 7.5 million to staff employed 2014–2021, the claim grounds, and that the attorney general supported the settlement. Status: closed by settlement. The plaintiff's lawyers' allegations of pressure on the plaintiff were left out, because they are allegations rather than findings.
- **No indictments, convictions or official inquiries** for Goldknopf, Tessler or Roth were found in Hebrew and English searches, Knesset records, or ToI, JDN and Israel Hayom coverage.
- Wedding-dance video (Mar 23, 2025) [8] and Makor Rishon remarks (July 2025) [19]: political criticism only (calls for his removal; Smotrich's response). No official act was found. Kept as context with neutral wording.

## Notes for the consistency review
- Many positions rest on the 2022 Likud–UTJ coalition agreement, which UTJ signed as a whole, and on Goldknopf's statements. There is no Agudat Yisrael platform and no per-MK vote data for the 25th Knesset. Closest-option lines for cost of living, crime, judicial system and Judea and Samaria are therefore thinly party-specific. The document says so.
- Several quoted figures (Porush, Eichler) now belong to Chomat Torat Yisrael. The document flags this, but the reviewer should check that Chomat Torat Yisrael's document does not double-count the same evidence without a similar caveat.
- The legal section links Goldknopf to an organization's official findings rather than to findings against him personally. The wording keeps that distinction explicit. A reviewer should compare it with how other parties' documents treat organization-level findings.
- Most sources are JDN, a Haredi news site. It was reliable on facts checked against other sources, but its framing ("persecution", "decree") appears in quotes only. Keep those as attributed quotes.
- [21] (ToI) names Tessler as possibly abstaining, while [62] (JDN) names only Goldknopf. Both are cited as reports, not as party decisions.
