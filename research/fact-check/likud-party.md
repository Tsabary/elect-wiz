## Legal section verification
- **Netanyahu criminal trial (ongoing).** The three cases, the charges (fraud and breach of trust in all three, bribery in Case 4000), his denial, and his testimony (10 Dec 2024 to 24 Jun 2026, 98 hearings) are confirmed by ToI [86]. The status as of 6 Sep 2026 comes from JPost reporting the court's proceedings [95]: defense witnesses for Netanyahu and three co-defendants; a panel headed by Judge Rivka Friedman-Feldman; five hearing days a week from 4 Oct; the judges' remark on the Case 4000 bribery charge; no verdict. No later development was found.
- **Pardon request (pending).** Filed in November 2025. The Justice Ministry pardons department recommended against. Minister Eliyahu's position paper in favour was submitted on 24 Mar 2026. Herzog's legal adviser (Michal Tzuk) is preparing a recommendation (ToI [87]). Herzog's official statement of 26 Apr 2026 says a plea agreement should be tried first (Reuters via Al-Monitor [96]). Searches found no later decision.
- **Submarine affair state commission (ongoing).** Chaired by retired Supreme Court President Grunis. It covers 2009–2016. Netanyahu received a warning letter in June 2024. The quoted grounds and the hearings scheduled for 1 Nov–31 Dec 2026 are confirmed (JPost, 26 Jul 2026 [88]).
- **ICC arrest warrant (outstanding).** The ICC's official defendant page (archived 6 Oct 2026 [97]) gives the warrant date (21 Nov 2024), the charges and the status "At large". The ICC press release of 21 Nov 2024 [98] confirms that the Pre-Trial Chamber rejected Israel's challenges to jurisdiction. Secondary reports mention later Appeals Chamber proceedings on jurisdiction, but these weren't verified from an official ICC source and are not included.
- **State Comptroller's October 7 review (frozen).** The Supreme Court froze it in December 2025 (ToI, 6 Feb 2026 [67]).
- **Haim Katz conviction (closed).** Plea agreement approved on 7 Feb 2022: six-month suspended sentence, NIS 75,000 fine, and the prosecution's statement on no moral turpitude and no corruption (Maariv [90]). The offence wording ("באמצעים אסורים") matches.

## Joint-list and split-history verification
- **CEC filing [2]** (the IDI-hosted copy of the gov.il page, published 08.09.2026 and updated 09.09.2026), checked against `content/registry/lists.json` and `parties.json`. The submitting parties are "הליכוד, תנועה לאומית ליברלית" and "תקווה חדשה הימין הממלכתי", matching the registry `memberPartyIds` [likud-party, new-hope]. The page title reads "הליכוד עם בנימין נתניהו לראשות הממשלה", while the registry uses "…בהנהגת…" from the approved-letters announcement. This discrepancy is already recorded in `research/ground-truth.md`. Ballot letters מחל match the registry. Top five (Netanyahu, Eli Cohen, Ohana, Levin, Regev) match `topCandidates`.
- **Candidate details.** #9 is "גואילי טלי", filed for New Hope. English reporting (ToI [6], JC [93]) names her Talik Gvili. Gila Gamliel is #25, May Golan #40, Idit Silman #46. Michel Buskila is not among the 120 names. Sa'ar (#7) and Elkin (#32) are filed for Likud. The only New Hope-filed candidates are #9, #88 and #89.
- **Split history.** Checked against IDI's New Hope profile [8] and ToI [9], [10], [94]: Sa'ar left Likud in Dec 2020 after a failed primary challenge; New Hope won 6 seats in 2021; it merged into National Unity in July 2022 and split away on 12 Mar 2024; it joined the government on 29 Sep 2024 (Globes [11]); merger agreement 13 Mar 2025 (Ynetnews [12]); approved by Likud's Central Committee 13 Aug 2025 [9].
- **Current status as of today.** Per Maariv's report of the Supreme Court ruling of 9 Aug 2026 [91], on 14 Jul 2026 the faction heads replaced the Knesset merger with a joint run on one list. The court rejected Haskel's petition and accepted this. This matches what the New Hope research agent reported. The Knesset member API, queried today, still shows the four-member "הימין הממלכתי" faction (Elkin, Buskila, Haskel, Hasson) and a 32-member Likud faction. Haskel resigned as deputy FM after the 14 Jul vote and founded Israel First (ToI [13]; JPost [14]).

## Notes for the consistency review
- The CEC spells #9 "טלי גואילי", while all English reporting found says "Talik Gvili". The doc uses Talik and notes the CEC spelling. The Hebrew edition should probably follow the CEC.
- Ynetnews [21] said Sa'ar's slot was "in the top five", but he is #7 on the CEC filing. Ynetnews also named Buskila as the second New Hope slot, but he is absent. These may be pre-filing reports. The doc states both facts without resolving them.
- The four Contradictions (Haredi enlistment, sovereignty votes, Gaza, 2018 surrogacy) were checked against their sources and are supported. The Gaza and sovereignty ones were reworded so that the government's side, and the context (Board of Peace letter; Trump's statement the next day), sit next to each other. The Gaza one is the weakest, because the withdrawals were explicitly conditional. The consistency reviewer may want to judge whether it should stay a Contradiction or become a plain note.
- Several "Action" items are government-wide actions led by coalition partners (Smotrich's finance policy, Ben Gvir's death-penalty law, Rothman's attorney-general law). The document attributes them, but the reviewer should check that this is handled the same way across coalition parties.
- Source [7] (Washington Examiner) and [47] (Jewish Virtual Library hosting a PMO statement) are not primary Israeli sources. They were verified and kept.
- The "Statement" items from likud.org.il are campaign claims (inflation, enlistment numbers and so on). They are tagged Statement and attributed.
