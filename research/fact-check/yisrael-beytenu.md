# Fact-check: yisrael-beytenu

- Document checked: content/research/en/yisrael-beytenu.md (researchedAsOf 2026-10-07)
- Checked by: Stage 3 agent (not the Stage 2 author) on 2026-10-07
- Claims checked: 196 · Corrected: 41 · Removed: 5 · Sources replaced: 4 (6, 37, 53, 76 in the new numbering); 2 sources removed (old 67 C14, old 68 Arutz Sheva); 4 sources added (79–82)
- Limited-information flag: false (the party has a Knesset and government record going back to 1999, six sitting MKs whose votes appear in Knesset open data, a published platform updated in October 2026, a 2025 document of principles, a 2026 economic plan and wide Hebrew and English coverage)

All 80 original sources were opened. News pages were read with WebFetch, and with curl plus HTML-to-text extraction for exact Hebrew wording and publication dates (Walla, Ynet, Globes). The CEC PDF was downloaded and parsed with pdftotext, and all five Knesset OData person queries were fetched as JSON. Votes were checked against the Knesset OData V4 service (KNS_PlenumVote / KNS_PlenumVoteResult) **by following every `@odata.nextLink` page**. That service returns at most 100 result rows per page, and a first-page-only read gave wrong totals (for example 55–45 instead of 63–52 for Basic Law: Torah Study, and 58–41 instead of 62–48 for the death penalty law). Every tally and per-MK vote now in the document was re-checked against the full, paginated result set or against the per-MK filtered query (fewer than 100 rows). Final paginated tallies: 43884 judicial selection 67–1 (2 present), 0 YB rows; 44575 Ma'ale Adumim 31–9–1, all 6 YB for; 45441 rabbinical arbitration 65–41 (1 present), all 6 YB against; 45858 death penalty 62–48–1, YB: Liberman, Forer, Malinovsky and Sova for, no record for Amar or Nir; 46248 Basic Law: Torah Study 63–52, all 6 YB against; 46670 Security Service Law amendment 28, 58–54, all 6 YB against; 46355 kashrut repeal 46–41, all 6 YB against; 13823 (12 May 2010) 9–49, 9 YB against; 16236 (27 July 2011) 17–40 (1 present), 7 YB against and Mutz-Matalon for; 13639 (15 March 2010, civil union bill, 2nd reading s.1) 46–14, all 13 YB MKs present voted for.

## Legal section verification
- **Liberman indictment, 30 Dec 2012:** Walla [73] reports that the amended indictment for fraud and breach of trust was filed in the Jerusalem Magistrate's Court after Liberman said he wouldn't seek immunity. AG Weinstein had sent the draft to the Knesset speaker. Liberman's resignation as foreign minister on 18 Dec 2012 is from the Knesset record [4].
- **Acquittal, 6 Nov 2013:** Haaretz [74] (Jerusalem Magistrate's Court, three judges, unanimous, the prosecution didn't prove the facts) and Walla [75].
- **No appeal:** JTA, 18 Dec 2013 [76]. The AG announced he wouldn't appeal. This replaces Mako's report of a prosecutors' recommendation. Status: final.
- **Kirshenbaum (decision: keep).** She was a Yisrael Beytenu MK, deputy interior minister and the official in charge of its coalition funds, and the case is the "Yisrael Beytenu affair" involving party funds. That makes it relevant to the party, but it is clearly framed as a former official who is not a current candidate or leader. Official acts: the Tel Aviv District Court sentence of 14 July 2021 (Ynet [77], which quotes Judge Yaron Levy) and the Supreme Court ruling of 24 Aug 2022 (ToI [79]: appeal unanimously rejected, sentence reduced to 7.5 years). ToI states that Liberman "has not been named a suspect in the case." She is not on the 2026 CEC list [1].
- **Other top candidates:** I spot-checked Hebrew searches on Amar, Forer, Sova, Malinovsky and Ben Shitrit with investigation and indictment terms. No official proceeding was found. One press report on a harassment complaint concerning Ben Shitrit as mayor was found (a journalist's report and a Press Council matter), but no police, prosecution or court act, so it was correctly left out under the official-sources rule.

This matches `lists.json` (letters ל, `memberPartyIds` [yisrael-beytenu]) and ground-truth.md row 11.
- It's a single-party list, so there's no split history for 2026. The historical joint runs are now stated correctly: NU faction during the 15th Knesset, a joint list with NU in 2003, and Likud Beytenu in 2013, per IDI [2] and Knesset faction names [4]. Liberman's faction record shows "הליכוד - ישראל ביתנו" until 9 July 2014, when the factions split.
