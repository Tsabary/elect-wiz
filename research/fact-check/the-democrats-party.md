# Fact-check: the-democrats-party

- Document checked: content/research/en/the-democrats-party.md (researchedAsOf 2026-10-06)
- Checked by: Stage 3 agent (not the Stage 2 author) on 2026-10-07
- Claims checked: 165 · Corrected: 29 · Removed: 4 · Sources replaced: 1 (source 65's query widened; 8 sources added: 71–78)
- Limited-information flag: false (detailed 16-page August 2026 platform, a four-MK faction with a full 25th-Knesset bill record, government experience through Labor and Meretz in 2021–2022, and wide Hebrew and English news coverage)

All 70 original sources were opened. News pages were read with WebFetch (or curl where a site blocked WebFetch). The four PDFs (CEC list page [1], IDI review [15], August 2026 platform [18], party constitution [19]) were downloaded and read in full with pdftotext. All ten Knesset OData queries [61]–[70] were fetched as JSON, and every bill claim was checked for date, status and initiators. A separate per-bill KNS_BillInitiator check was run on all 45 bills cited.

## Legal section verification
- **Kariv police investigation (new):** JNS (April 8, 2025) reports that Kariv was questioned by Lahav 433 as a suspect over the leak of a June 13, 2023 classified FADC briefing, after a complaint by Speaker Ohana, and gives his denial [74]. Ynetnews (July 9, 2025) reports the police statement that the investigation was complete and the file sent to the State Attorney's Economic Department "for review and decision" [75]. JDN (July 9, 2026) reports Lavi's High Court petition asking for a decision without delay, which says no decision has been made [76]. A search for a later decision found none. Status: pending.
- **Golan, 2023 "refusal is a legitimate tool" remark (new):** Israel Hayom (July 29, 2025) reports the Deputy State Attorney's office decided not to order a criminal investigation, and quotes its reasoning [72]. Earlier July 2024 reports (Mako, JDN, ICE) describe the referral to police, but they differ on whether it was an "examination" or an "investigation". Only the final decision is stated.
- **May 2024 remarks:** police statement quoted from ToI [57]. Kept.
- **CEC disqualification bid rejected 14–2 on Sept 24, 2026:** ToI [22]; matches research/ground-truth.md. Kept.
- **Katz order (2025):** ToI [23, 25]. An official act by the defense minister. Wording expanded to the order's full terms.
- **Likud CEC complaint (Aug 25, 2026):** JPost [58]. No decision found. Kept.
- **Shin Bet protection petition:** JPost Oct 6, 2026 [59]: no ruling; the court asked for updates. Kept as open.
- **Defamation suit v. Sara Netanyahu (new):** JPost Sept 10, 2026 [73]: filed in the Central District Court for NIS 2.55m. Pending.
- **State Comptroller:** I searched the mevaker.gov.il library. The only hit is the Nov 2025 national-security-concept report, which describes Golan's 2017–2018 committee work and makes no finding against him [78]. A gov.il search for AG and State Attorney announcements found nothing about these candidates. A Hebrew and English news search on Lazimi, Kariv, Rayten, Fink and Lasky turned up only the Kariv file. The Lazimi "arson" claim in a JDN piece is a political allegation by MK Sukkot with no official basis, so it wasn't added.

## Joint-list and split-history verification
- **CEC filing [1] (IDI copy, read in full):** submitted "מטעם מפלגת הדמוקרטים מיסודה של תנועת העבודה ומטעם מפלגת מרצ". There are 120 candidates: 119 are filed for the Democrats, and only #6 Gaby Lasky (לסקי שוץ גבריאלה) is filed for Meretz. Rozin #8, Zandberg #109 and Galon #116 are filed for the Democrats. Merav Michaeli isn't on the list (#82 is a different Michaeli, Ido). This matches the registry (`the-democrats`, letters אמת, memberPartyIds the-democrats-party + meretz) and ground-truth.md.
- **Ballot letters אמת:** Ynet, CEC publication of Sept 27, 2026 [2]. The list name "הדמוקרטים בראשות יאיר גולן" is the CEC page title [1].
- **IDI [15]:** places 6, 8 and 14 were reserved for Meretz; 112,376 eligible voters; turnout over 86%; Golan may place 2 candidates in every ten places. ToI [16] gives "97,000 of its 112,376 registered members".
- **Party constitution [19]:** the Labor Party constitution, updated July 2025, published on democrats.org.il as "חוקת הדמוקרטים". It guarantees two former Meretz members on the elections committee under the agreement approved by the party convention on 12.7.24. Confirmed.
- **2020 split history:** ToI (Jan 13, 2020) confirms the Labor-Gesher + Meretz joint run [13]. Golan's Knesset positions record [10] shows the faction "העבודה - גשר - מרץ" from 2020-03-16, "העבודה - מרץ" from 2020-03-23 and "מרץ" from 2020-04-06. Globes (Apr 6, 2020) confirms Peretz's request to dissolve the merger, his quote, and the Peretz/Shmuli pledge not to serve under an indicted PM [14]. All confirmed.
- **2022:** ToI [12] confirms that the parties ran separately, that Labor went from 7 to 4 seats, and that Meretz fell about 3,800 votes short.
- **No split of the current arrangement** was found.

## Notes for the consistency review
- **The February 2, 2026 "Contradiction" was reclassified.** It was two same-day statements by Golan, the second narrowing the first. It wasn't an action contradicting a position. If Stage 4 applies Contradiction tags to statement-vs-statement cases elsewhere, align the approach.
- **The two-state position now rests on Statements:** Golan's July 2026 podcast (reported by Arutz Sheva [77], a right-leaning outlet, though the quote itself is plain), Kariv via JPost [20], and Golan's settlement-blocs remark [24]. The platform doesn't say "two states". Keep the tier at Statement.
- **Several votes are described as "the opposition" voting or boycotting.** The Torah-study Basic Law, the Judicial Selection Committee law and the October 7 commission bill have no per-party vote records (Knesset vote data wasn't used). Wording now follows the sources.
- **The Kariv investigation is still pending** and should be rechecked before publication.
- **Source 65 is a paginated OData result** (over 100 rows). The cited initiators appear across its pages. I checked them with per-bill queries.
- **Neutral wording:** quotes such as "draft evasion", "whitewash commission" and "settler terror" are attributed quotes from Golan or the platform. The narrative uses glossary terms ("applying sovereignty", "Judea and Samaria").
