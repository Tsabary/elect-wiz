## Legal section verification
- **Civil Service Commission inquiry, 2007–2008.** Globes, 13 Nov 2007 [71]: the CSC summoned Zelekha for questioning over the conflict-of-interest arrangement he signed (Derech Eretz/Africa Israel-related tenders, light-rail meetings, consultants in the Bank Leumi share distribution); CSC spokesman Arye Greenblatt confirmed "there is currently an examination". Globes, 3 Apr 2008 [72]: the CSC discipline department (headed by Adv. Asaf Rosenberg) found he acted contrary to his arrangement in three matters (Route 431, light-rail meetings, Bank Leumi privatisation), and Commissioner Shmuel Hollander passed the material to State Attorney Moshe Lador. Both are reputable reports of official acts. **Outcome:** searched in Hebrew (Globes, Walla finance/news, general web) for a State Attorney decision, disciplinary charge or court ruling; none found. Walla items on Route 431 (2009–2010) cover only compensation to Danya Cebus and do not mention Zelekha. Status recorded as "could not be verified (October 2026)".
- **Deputy AG decision, 20 Dec 2018.** Srugim [40] (dated 20 Dec 2018 in metadata) reports Deputy AG Dina Zilber's letter to Kahlon upholding Finance Ministry legal adviser Assi Messing's position that Zelekha could not head the committee on price rises because of multiple conflicts of interest (adviser to the Histadrut under Avi Nissenkorn; adviser to three workers' committees at large food-retail chains; other bodies dealing with the Treasury), and that the decision casts no doubt on him personally. Zelekha said he would petition the High Court; no ruling found. The Israel Hayom interview [26] (Sep 2026) confirms the 2018 episode from Zelekha's side. Official act, reported by a reputable outlet. Kept.
- **Context items (not proceedings against Zelekha):** witness in the Bank Leumi inquiry (Ynetnews 30 Oct 2006 [31], quoting the comptroller's office); whistleblower request (Ynetnews 3 Sep 2007 [32]); temporary protection order issued 10 Oct 2007 (Bizportal [73]). All verified.

## Notes for the consistency review
- **Change over time labels:** all four (run alone → joint run; earlier criticism of Hendel; 2,000 → "500 or 800" yeshiva exemptions; 2021 vs current platform on enlistment and Judea and Samaria) are statement-versus-statement or platform-versus-platform, so `**Change over time:**` is right. No `**Contradiction:**` is warranted: the party has no actions of its own. Zelekha's post-Treasury paid advice to large workers' committees (Globes [34]) sits uneasily with his stated opposition to committees in monopolies, but it is professional work, not a political action against a stated position, so it is reported as background, not as a Contradiction.
- **Tiers:** Zelekha's ice columns, interviews and the 2023 committee remarks are all tagged Statement (the committee remarks as "Statement (formal setting)"). Platform pages are Formal commitment. No Action-tier claims exist. Factual events (2007 dispute, protection order, CSC inquiry, 2018 decision, car committee) all rest on independent news sources; the party's site is used only for "the party says" claims and the CV.
- **Thin issues:** crime in Arab society (2021 platform only), religion and state (kashrut, family law, gender segregation; nothing on civil marriage, conversion or Shabbat transport), Judea and Samaria (no sovereignty position), Iran (columns only; nothing on Hezbollah/Lebanon). Closest lines are now hedged accordingly.
- **Housing:** the Globes fact-check [57] rates his "prices fell only in my years" claim "not true"; the party's rebuttal is noted. The index also rose 0.8% over his full term per Globes; not added, as the document already summarises the verdict.
- **Party-side framing:** the FAQ page [35] is combative toward named critics; the document uses it only for the party's own claims, attributed.
- **Leader's role in the Bank Leumi affair:** worded as "reported to be a key witness" [31]; Globes English (30 Aug 2007) says he "disclosed to the State Comptroller" Olmert's alleged involvement, but the document's more cautious wording was kept.
