## Legal section verification
- **2000 police recommendation / 2003 decision:** Ynet, 25 July 2000 [66], reports the police recommendation and that the file went to the State Attorney. Mako/N12, 13 September 2019 [67], opened in full, reports that in 2003 the State Attorney closed the case: AG Rubinstein and State Attorney Arbel "view with severity" the acts, but the evidence "was not free of doubts regarding the mental element," and much time had passed, so they did not ask the Knesset to lift immunity. The official decision is stated, so the item stays. Status: closed without indictment.
- **State Comptroller 1997–1998:** Globes, 20 May 1997 [62] (Comptroller told the State Control Committee she was considering referral to the AG) and Globes, 6 May 1998 [63] (annual report quote "The parade of political and flawed appointments is led, by a wide margin, by… Meir Porush"; 8 of 25 cases). These are State Comptroller findings, an official act.
- **1998 High Court of Justice ruling:** Globes, 13 October 1998 [64]: a seven-justice panel unanimously accepted the Histadrut's petition and cancelled Einstein's appointment, effective in six months. Court ruling, kept.
- **2017 permits committee:** the primary document, State Comptroller decision 4/2017 of 7 December 2017 [68], was opened and confirms the refusal. Court follow-up (interim order refused, resignation) and the Deputy AG (Zilber) opinion come from Calcalist, 7 May 2018 [76]. The Zman Yisrael claim that the petition was "rejected" is not supported by the original and was not used.
- **1998 police recommendation [65]:** removed (see above).
- **Boyan expulsion (2007, #leadership):** a Hasidic-court matter, not a legal proceeding. Ynet [69] confirms it was over the Beitar Illit mayoral race. The Zman profile wrongly says Beit Shemesh; the document follows the original.
- Stark: no proceedings found. Nothing in the section comes from a rival's complaint.
