- **2008 District Court appeal:** Arutz Sheva, 17 Sept 2008 [59]. **AP "convicted eight times":** AP via WSLS, 18 July 2024 [9].
- **2012 acquittal:** Walla, 13 Mar 2012 (Judge Alexander Ron, Jerusalem Magistrate's Court) [60].
- **High Court petitions to remove Ben Gvir (pending):** Maariv, 18 Mar 2026 (order nisi in February; panel of nine; hearing moved to 15 April) [37]; JDN, 16 Apr 2026 (interim orders, quoted from the decision) [35]; Bizportal/Takdin, 20 July 2026 (AG update notice to the court) [34]; Walla, 5 Aug 2026 (government response) [36]. Searches on 7 Oct 2026 (Hebrew and English) found no final ruling. Ynetnews (6 July 2026) also lists the case as pending.
- **Knesset Ethics Committee reprimand, 23 June 2025:** N12 [61].
- **Gotliv indictment and immunity:** JPost 9 and 15 June 2026 (indictment filed by the AG in May 2026; House Committee 11–3) [40, 62]; Walla 17 June 2026 (plenum 61–48 and 62–48; Shin Bet employee's petition) [63]. Maariv and Kikar confirm the petition was filed. No ruling on it found as of 7 Oct 2026.
- **Dorfman:** Kikar HaShabbat, 4 Feb 2025 (questioned at the PIID; lawyer's denial) [65]; Walla, 9 July 2026 (questioned under caution) [64]; Maariv, 28 Sept 2025, quoting the PIID's official statement (questioned again under caution) [76]. No decision found as of 7 Oct 2026. Maariv's headline also says he refused to cooperate and an arrest warrant was issued. That claim is not in the PIID statement and is not used in the text.
- **Death penalty petitions:** policy matter. They are in #other-death-penalty, not #legal, as required.
