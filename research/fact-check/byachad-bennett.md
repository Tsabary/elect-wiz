## Legal section verification
- **State Comptroller fine, 2013 (NIS 65,000).** The Algemeiner (Aug 22, 2013), crediting Ynet, reports State Comptroller Joseph Shapira's report on party primaries. It says Bennett exceeded the spending ceiling, did not list all expenses (private-investigator fees about MK Nissan Slomiansky during the Jewish Home primaries) and did not provide authorisations. The Ynetnews original (ynetnews.com/article/4420654) was also opened and gives the same facts, but it shows no date, so the dated Algemeiner piece is cited [77]. Reputable reporting of an official act. Status: concluded (fine).
- **State Comptroller fine, Dec 2, 2015 (NIS 30,000).** Kipa reports the Comptroller's report on the Dec 2014–Jan 2015 primaries. It says expenses exceeded the legal limit and income exceeded the legal limits [78]. Status: concluded.
- The official mevaker.gov.il PDF (2016.10.18-ShlomoRaz-Mimun.pdf) redirected to the homepage, and web.archive.org cannot be fetched by this tool, so the official text itself could not be opened. Both items rest on news reporting of the official reports, which the Stage 3 rules allow.
- **High Court ruling, May 18, 2022 (Ynet [73]).** Verified. Justice Alroni wrote "a bleak picture of clearly improper management". The petitions (Ra'anana municipality and neighbours) were partly accepted and the noise rules were corrected. There was no personal finding against Bennett. Status: decided.
- No indictments or convictions were found for Bennett, Terner Eyal or Avishar Ben-Horin.

## Joint-list and split-history verification
- List name ביחד בראשות נפתלי בנט; submitted on behalf of ביחד בראשות בנט מחזירים את התקווה and יש עתיד - בראשות יאיר לפיד. Candidate order 1–17 matches the doc, checked against the CEC page text (IDI copy, published 07.09.2026) [11], `content/registry/lists.json` and `research/ground-truth.md`.
- Ballot letters רק: confirmed by Ynet's report of the CEC ballot announcement [76] and matching the registry.
- No prior joint run by Bennett and Lapid: IDI [2] ("They have never run together under one list").
- Bennett's past splits: he left The Jewish Home in late 2018 [2]. Knesset OData shows he moved from the joint Yamina faction (1077, Oct 3–10, 2019) to The New Right faction (941, from Oct 10, 2019) [15, 79]. The 2022 coalition collapse and Knesset dissolution on June 30, 2022, are confirmed by [3, 9].
- Beliak's (Yesh Atid) "technical bloc / joint faction after the election" statement is verified [14]. Yesh Atid has about 40% of the slots and 12 of its 24 MKs are expected on the slate [10].

## Notes for the consistency review
- Party-plan pages (be-yahad.org.il) carry no dates. Their content was verified as of 2026-10-06/07.
- The 30% crime-fall figure is a party claim that could not be verified. The doc attributes it explicitly in both places where it appears.
- The Jerusalem Post [20] swaps the backgrounds of Terner Eyal and Avishar Ben-Horin. Ynetnews [18] and ToI [8] agree with each other, and the doc follows them. The Party Registrar notice [75] also lists Liran Avishar Ben-Horin among the party's founders.
- Ynetnews [18] numbers candidates by internal party rank (Terner No. 2 etc.), not by joint-list position. The doc uses CEC list positions. This is consistent.
- Source titles [39], [40] ("draft dodgers") and [49] ("West Bank") keep the outlets' wording. The body text uses glossary terms throughout.
- The party's website has plans (Haredi integration, education, government, public service, transport, small business, tourism, women, early childhood, Israel's global standing) that the doc does not cite. These are optional extra depth for "other positions".
- The Gantz claim about Bennett opposing term-limit legislation in 2021 is included only as Gantz's attributed statement. A Knesset vote record check could turn it into a formal Contradiction.
- [1] (ToI liveblog) also includes poll figures. None were carried into the doc.
