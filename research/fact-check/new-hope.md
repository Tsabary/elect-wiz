## Legal section verification
- **No indictments, convictions or official inquiries against Sa'ar or Elkin:** no contrary report found in the searches made for this check. Kept.
- **The High Court annulled the June 2026 comptroller vote over ballot secrecy and ordered a new vote:** this is a court ruling. Confirmed by ToI [61] and JPost [67] (ruling in July 2026, the "Selfiegate" filming).
- **Rabello has not taken office:** ToI [61] says he refused to take office; JPost [67] says his entry is frozen. Worded neutrally as "has not taken office".
- **Haskel's affidavit:** ToI [62], 23 July 2026, reports her affidavit to the High Court. **Attribution to Buskila:** ToI [61], citing Kan, about her statement to police.
- **Sa'ar's counter-affidavit ("baseless"; told members not to film):** ToI [61].
- **The AG directed Lahav 433 to examine the conflicting affidavits for possible perjury:** an official act, ToI [61]. Ynetnews (undated, not cited) adds that the AG and State Attorney asked police to check whether the Likud or Haskel filed a false affidavit, and that a criminal investigation "could" be opened depending on her testimony. This confirms it is an examination, not a criminal investigation of a named suspect.
- **Current status:** the latest report found is JPost, 19 August 2026 [67]. The new vote had not been held, and Speaker Ohana said there would be none. No later report of a criminal investigation, an indictment or a new vote was found (Hebrew and English searches on 7 October 2026). Note: this matter concerns the party's MKs (Haskel, Buskila) and an affidavit by Sa'ar. No leader is a suspect.
