## Legal section verification
- **Winter – MAG decision (closed).** Srugim, 15 Aug 2018 [60], reproduces the Military Advocate General's announcement that no criminal investigation would be opened into the 1 Aug 2014 Rafah battle. Official act, reputable report. The decision names no commander. ToI (3 May 2023) [61] on Winter's command and the IDF's internal finding. Status: closed.
- **Winter – Chief of Staff reprimand (9 June 2021).** Ynetnews, 9 June 2021 [72]: Lt. Gen. Aviv Kochavi reprimanded Brig. Gen. Ofer Winter for an unauthorized personal conversation with Yamina chairman Naftali Bennett. An official IDF disciplinary act, reported the same day. Jewish Insider [64] gives the context (98th Division commander, rule against unapproved meetings). Kept; flagged as disciplinary, not criminal.
- **Winter – Military Advocate's Office letter (June 2024).** JDN, 19 June 2024 [63], quoting the letter from the Military Advocate's Office to his lawyer. Official act. Kept.
- **Haddad – indictment (pending).** Indictment filed 7 Aug 2025 by the Tel Aviv District Prosecutor's Office in the Tel Aviv Magistrate's Court for a reckless and negligent act with a firearm: Ynet [73], Mako/N12 [70] and Maariv [69], all quoting the indictment; the scooter rider was charged with assault. House arrest extended by the Tel Aviv Magistrate's Court on 4 Aug 2025 [68, 70]. Current status: N12, 28 Aug 2026 [74] (one hearing before Judge Yaron Gat; request to cancel the indictment; advanced plea negotiations; next hearing early October 2026). Searches (Hebrew and English) found no report of a plea agreement, ruling or closure as of 7 Oct 2026. Status: pending.
- **Haddad – threats against him.** JDN [24] reports threats as the party described them; kept as "as a victim" context, not an official proceeding. It isn't an allegation against a leader.
- No complaints by rivals or "police examining" items appear in the section.

## Notes for the consistency review
- The Channel 12 report on Winter's October 2023 advice rests on one unnamed former military official, plus a claim by a named rival (Shaul Meridor, Yashar). Kept because Winter partly confirms proposing an alternative plan; worded with both sides and a note that no official record exists. A reviewer may prefer to cut it if the corpus standard for unnamed-source reports is stricter.
- The "no Palestinian state" position isn't in the founding document [2]; every instance is tiered **Statement** (launch speech, party written answers to Ynet/Arutz Sheva, Winter's statements).
- Glossary: "State of Palestine" (Haddad's quote) and "independent commission" (founding-document quote) appear only inside quotation marks. "deserter" appears in Winter's account of a specific arrest (one individual, as the glossary allows).
- The Haddad interview date is reported inconsistently (2020 per Haddad himself, 2021 per Israel Hayom, "more than five years ago" per JPost); the document gives all three.
- Jewish Insider [54] says Haddad's open indictment "would likely bar him from serving as a cabinet minister"; N12 [74] says the Attorney General would have to examine it. The document doesn't include either assessment (not an official act).
- Several of the "Formal commitment" items come from the party's website founding document [2], which isn't dated; it was fetched on 7 Oct 2026 and matched every quote.
