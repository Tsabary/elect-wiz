## Notes for the consistency review
- **Contradiction #3 decision:** re-cast as "Mixed messages from the spiritual leader" (context, not `**Contradiction:**`). Rabbi Yitzhak Yosef is not a candidate, his statements are not actions, and his office denied the Army Radio report. The denial is stated.
- **Contradictions #1 and #2:** verified and kept, but narrowed. #1 now pairs the January 2025 statements (with the same-day retraction) against the October 22, 2025 committee vote. #2 pairs the January 4, 2026 budget vow (by party spokesman Asher Medina) against the March 30, 2026 budget support.
- **Cost of living:** the VAT rise is now tied to Shas only as a member of the government that decided it, with a Shas MK's recorded opposition in committee; no MK-level vote record exists in open data. Other reviewers should apply the same standard to other coalition parties.
- **Crime in Arab society:** the new evidence is a single 2023 ministerial action about municipal funding, not a crime policy. The Closest option is hedged; a reviewer may prefer to treat it as "no documented position" for matching purposes.
- **Judea and Samaria:** the July 2025 declaration's operative text covers "areas of Jewish settlement", so the Closest option moved from `full-sovereignty` to `partial-sovereignty-area-c`. Other parties' documents citing the same declaration should be checked for consistent wording.
- **Iran/Lebanon** now rests on Deri's own statements (Israel Hayom, Kikar HaShabbat, JDN) and his part in security cabinet discussions; still no formal platform.
- **Dror Amos challenge:** ground-truth.md asks to recheck that no appeal was filed against the CEC chair's Sept 23 decision. No report of an appeal was found in this check, but no dedicated search of Supreme Court appeals was made.
- **Source quality:** many items come from Kikar HaShabbat and other Haredi outlets (reliable for party quotes, but sympathetic in tone). The Ynet article (17) uses loaded headline wording; only Deri's quoted words are used.
- **Vote breakdowns:** the 25th Knesset votes by party (2023 reasonableness, 2025 judges law, 2025 sovereignty declaration, 2026 inquiry bill) are inferred from "all coalition MKs" reporting, not from roll calls.
