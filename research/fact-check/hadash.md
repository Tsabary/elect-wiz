Method: every cited URL was opened. The 2013 platform PDF was text-extracted and searched for each figure. The Knesset member pages (main.knesset.gov.il) and Al Jazeera were blocked to all fetch methods.

## Legal section verification
- **Cassif expulsion motion (2024):** process under Basic Law: The Knesset s.42A(c), 90 votes needed, initiated by MK Oded Forer (JPost [28]); plenum vote 40436 on 19 Feb 2024: 85 for, 11 against, 1 present; Odeh, Touma-Sliman, Cassif, Atauna (and Tibi) against (Knesset open data [29], 97 rows). Outcome: failed; Cassif still an MK (KNS_PersonToPosition [100]). Official Knesset proceeding.
- **Ethics Committee suspensions:** 11 Nov 2024 (unanimous, six months, may vote, two weeks' pay) [26]; 9 July 2025 (19 Oct–19 Dec 2025, two weeks' pay, "systematic and consistent pattern") [27]. Official Knesset Ethics Committee decisions; both periods have ended.
- **Cassif candidacy:** CEC 2019 disqualification (15–10) [31], overturned by the Supreme Court [26]; CEC 23 Sept 2026, 19–5, Likud petition, AG and State Attorney opposed [13]; Supreme Court 2 Oct 2026, 7–2 (Mintz, Stein dissenting) [14]. Status: candidate.
- **Joint List candidacy:** CEC 23 Sept 2026, 18–5–1 on a Likud request [101]; Supreme Court unanimously overturned on 2 Oct 2026 [14] (also matches research/ground-truth.md).
- **Odeh expulsion motion (2025):** 14 July 2025, 73 of 90 required votes, failed [30]. Official Knesset proceeding.
- **Farah:** no official proceeding found in [9, 10]; nothing placed in #legal.
- No State Comptroller findings, indictments or convictions found for Jabareen, Ghattas, Odeh or Cassif.
