- No official proceedings found. Searched Hebrew and English (Kikar HaShabbat, Behadrei Haredim, JDN, general outlets) for investigations, indictments, State Comptroller findings, AG or court decisions involving Litner or the party: none.
- The donations statement is kept and labelled as the leader's own claim (verified against the 2 September 2026 Kikar HaShabbat interview [19]: "almost a million shekels already reported" on the State Comptroller's site).
- The 3 August 2026 "petition to halt the campaign" (party timeline [18] and its Hebrew press page) was removed: no court or CEC record could be found, and the only attribution is the party's own site.
