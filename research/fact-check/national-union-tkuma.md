## Legal section verification
- **2005 detention (Smotrich):** Ynetnews, 12 January 2015 [91]. It reports the arrest of five suspects (formal suspected offences listed), detention in a Shin Bet facility, a District Court rejecting the appeal against detention, and Supreme Court Justice Salim Joubran upholding it. Release after several weeks; "No charges were ultimately brought against him." These are official acts reported by a reputable outlet. Status: closed, no indictment.
- **Foreign sanctions (Smotrich), 10 June 2025:** Israel Hayom [92] quotes the five governments' official joint statement. Status was verified against the UK's official sanctions list CSV (FCDO), downloaded 7 Oct 2026 [97]: Smotrich is still listed (designated 10/06/2025, Global Human Rights Sanctions Regulations 2020; asset freeze, travel ban, director disqualification). Reasoning for keeping it: the corpus standard admits official acts by foreign authorities when clearly labelled. The Likud document keeps the ICC arrest warrant on the same basis, and this item is labelled as an act of foreign governments with no Israeli proceeding. The current status of the Canadian, Australian, New Zealand and Norwegian measures was not checked, and the text says so.
- **Ohad Tal (No. 12), Knesset Ethics Committee, 12 May 2026:** Israel Hayom (Hebrew) [93]. The committee found that Minister Karhi, Minister Ben-Gvir and MK Ohad Tal breached the duty to supervise their advisers, the first time it applied its decision of principle on this. A remark was imposed on Karhi; no sanction on Tal is mentioned. Status: decided.
- **CEC video ruling:** removed from #legal (see above).
- No indictments or convictions were found for Strock, Rothman or Mor in the sources reviewed. Feiglin was not researched here, as the document says.
