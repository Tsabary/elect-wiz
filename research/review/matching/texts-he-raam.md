## English texts questioned

judea-samaria / separation-two-states and two-states-pre-1967-border: both are labelled `agrees`, but each text says the research "cannot tell whether the party holds this option or" the other one. The two options conflict on substance: one keeps the large settlement blocs through land swaps, the other uses the exact pre-1967 border with every settlement evacuated. Read alone, in either language, the texts describe a shared two-state core with the border question open. That reads as partial agreement, not agreement, and calling both mutually exclusive options `agrees` gives a voter a definite match that the text itself denies. Suggested to Stage 7c: label both options `partially`, keeping the current wording, or keep `agrees` only if the research supports one of the two options and rewrite the text to say so. The Hebrew renders the current English faithfully, so it is not a Hebrew finding. Redo it after 7c.
