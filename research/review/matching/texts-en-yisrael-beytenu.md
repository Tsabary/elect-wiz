Observation on the 0.4 cases. Eight of this party's 41 labels are `partially` because of a single closeness of 0.4 against the main option: active-state-regulation, state-led-affordable-rental, develop-periphery-and-transport, advance-international-arrangement, separation-two-states, continue-judicial-changes, keep-current-system-stability, commission-chosen-by-neutral-mechanism. In two (state-led-affordable-rental, advance-international-arrangement) the party happens to have its own documented overlap and "partly agrees" reads naturally. In three (active-state-regulation, develop-periphery-and-transport, neutral mechanism) the text can only find thin or generic common ground. In three the research contradicts the label. The texts built on 0.5 and 0.6 (for example keep-status-quo at 0.5, full-sovereignty, gradual-targets, force-plus-agreements at 0.6) all read naturally as partial. Judgement: 0.4 ("the main thrust differs") reads as "disagrees, with some common ground", not as "partly agrees". Moving the `partially` cut-off above 0.4 (for example 0.45) would fix the three wrong labels here without touching the grid; the two natural partials would then need a "disagrees, though it shares X" text.
