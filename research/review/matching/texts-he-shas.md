## English texts questioned

- **housing / develop-periphery-and-transport (`disagrees`), as the author asked.** The text only contrasts focuses ("Your answer centres on … The party's documented housing measures centre on direct help for buyers"). It records no Shas opposition to investing outside the center. Read alone, in either language, it reads closer to "unclear" or "partly agrees" than "disagrees". Elsewhere in the same file, direct-help-for-buyers cites coalition grants of NIS 100,000 "for buyers in outlying areas", which goes some way toward the periphery option. My judgement: the label is not supported by its own text. Stage 7c should either cite evidence of disagreement or reconsider the label (likely `partially`). The Hebrew faithfully renders the English and is not at fault.
- **judea-samaria / partial-sovereignty-area-c (`agrees`, with "though the evidence is mixed"), as the author asked.** The July 2025 declaration (sovereignty over the areas of Jewish settlement, co-submitted, ten Shas MKs for) does match this option best among the six, and the text says so ("closest recorded position"). However, the text itself states that on binding sovereignty bills since 2023 Shas MKs "voted against or did not vote", and the neighbouring maintain-current-situation option is `partially` on that same evidence. An `agrees` that has to carry a "mixed" hedge is borderline. My judgement: defensible as the closest option, but Stage 7c should confirm whether `partially` fits better. The Hebrew ("אם כי המידע המתועד אינו עקבי") keeps the hedge and reads as agrees with a caveat, matching the English.
- **Roles "Welfare Minister" (Margi) and "Interior Minister" (Arbel)**, at the dates given: these were already in the checked English but are not supported by content/registry/ or the "Leadership and key candidates" section. Stage 7c should confirm them against the full research (findings #8, #9).
