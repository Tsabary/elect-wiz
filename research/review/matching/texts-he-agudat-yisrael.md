The leadership section says Tessler was Deputy Minister of Culture and Sport from January 2023 to July 2025 and "returned to the Knesset in July 2025", which means he was not an MK in March 2024. This wording was in the checked English before the Hebrew author's edit, not added by it. Stage 7c should confirm Tessler's role on that date and whether the vote was in the Knesset.
- judicial-system / continue-judicial-changes and keep-current-system-stability (low weight): these texts count Porush among the party's voters on 15 July 2026. The leadership section lists him as a "former Agudat Yisrael figure now outside the party" (now Chomat Torat Yisrael) and gives no date for when he left. The framing was already in the English before the Hebrew author's edit; Stage 7c may want to confirm he was still in the party on that date.
