# Consistency review: group 1

- Reviewed on 2026-10-12 by a Stage 4 group reviewer (not an author or fact-checker of these documents)
- Documents reviewed: likud-party, new-hope, jewish-national-front, eretz-yisrael-shelanu, national-union-tkuma, zehut, atid-echad

Method: all seven documents and their fact-check logs were read in full. Knesset votes were read from the official vote-details service (`www.knesset.gov.il/WebSiteApi/knessetapi/Votes/GetVoteDetails/{id}`, all MKs in one response, one request every 2.5 seconds) and, for bill records and initiators, from the OData service. 55 vote records were opened: every vote ID cited in new-hope (41), the Likud tallies below, the three opposition sovereignty bills, and the seven 10 May 2021 votes. Each tally was compared with the record's counters and with a count of its per-MK rows. Every new claim rests on a source opened today.
