- **yesh-atid, 6 July 2026 first reading (59–0).** Rests on a press figure because no official per-member record exists yet.
- **yesodot-yisrael legal section** keeps the defamation ruling in which Tropper was the plaintiff. It is a court ruling involving the leader and is described as such; see the question below.
