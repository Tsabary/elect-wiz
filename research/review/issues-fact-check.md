# Fact-check of approved issue texts (English)

- Date: 2026-10-06 (election: 27 October 2026)
- Checked text: `research/review/issues-final-approved-en.md` (owner-approved 2026-10-06)
- Method: every factual claim in each "More info" (and factual statements inside descriptions/options) was checked by opening pages with WebFetch. Search snippets were used only to find pages, never as evidence. Where a page was paywalled, blocked or timed out, another reputable page that states the same fact was opened instead and recorded. Wikipedia was used for orientation only.
- Scope: the 10 issues; plus every source marked [S] in §7 of `issues-review.md`.
- No other files were edited.

## 1. Summary of proposed corrections

Two corrections are needed (factually outdated or inaccurate). Three more are recommended as safer wording. Everything else was confirmed.

**Addendum (owner answers, 2026-10-06):** a third accuracy correction, C3 (Haredi "never served" → "most never served"), and two owner-decided wording changes (the October 7 government reason and the Hezbollah description) were applied after this check. See §5.

### Needed

**C1. gaza (More info)**
- Original: "The army still controls about half of Gaza."
- Proposed: "The army still controls more than half of Gaza."
- Reason: the ceasefire line (the "Yellow Line") started at about 53% of Gaza, but Israel has since moved it west. Reported figures in 2026: about 60% (ABC, 6 Jul 2026), about 70% according to defense officials (JPost, 23 Jun 2026), 65% (MSF, 5 Oct 2026). The Security Council Report (Oct 2026) says about 50% under the agreements, but with forces holding ground "beyond" the line. "More than half" is safe under all of these figures.
- Sources: https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-07-06/idf-controls-more-gaza-territory-as-israel-yellow-line-moves/106836640 ; https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/defense-news/article-900215 ; https://doctorswithoutborders.org/latest/three-years-israeli-forces-are-strangling-gaza ; https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/monthly-forecast/2026-10/the-middle-east-including-the-palestinian-question-26.php

**C2. october-7-inquiry (More info)**
- Original: "No investigation has been set up yet."
- Proposed: "No commission of inquiry has been set up yet."
- Reason: the IDF ran and published its own internal investigations into October 7 (presented 27 Feb 2025). So "no investigation" is too broad. What has not been set up is a commission of inquiry, whether a state commission or the government's proposed one. The government's bill passed only a first reading (7 Jul 2026).
- Sources: https://www.timesofisrael.com/idfs-oct-7-probes-show-it-misread-hamas-for-years-left-southern-israel-utterly-vulnerable/ ; https://www.timesofisrael.com/knesset-passes-controversial-politically-appointed-oct-7-probe-in-1st-reading/ ; https://www.timesofisrael.com/government-says-no-oct-7-inquiry-before-election-urges-high-court-not-to-intervene/

### Recommended (safer wording)

**R1. gaza (More info)**
- Original: "A ceasefire based on a US plan has held since October 2025, and the remains of the last hostage were returned in January 2026."
- Proposed: "A ceasefire based on a US plan has been in place since October 2025, and the remains of the last hostage were returned in January 2026."
- Reason: the ceasefire is formally in force, but Israel strikes Gaza often, including strikes in Khan Younis reported on 4 Oct 2026. About 1,394 Palestinians have been killed since the ceasefire began (SCR, late Sep 2026). Al Jazeera calls it a "nominal ceasefire" with "near-daily attacks". "Has held" overstates it, while "has been in place" is neutral and accurate.
- Sources: https://www.securitycouncilreport.org/monthly-forecast/2026-10/the-middle-east-including-the-palestinian-question-26.php ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/9/israel-rejects-trumps-15-point-plan-for-gaza ; https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-04-2026

**R2. iran-and-regional-security (More info)**
- Original: "Israel has pulled out of some areas in southern Lebanon, while fighting between the US and Iran continued into September 2026."
- Proposed: "Israel has handed some areas in southern Lebanon to the Lebanese army, while fighting between the US and Iran continued into September 2026."
- Reason: under the US-brokered framework (June 2026), Israel handed two "pilot zones" to the Lebanese army (20–21 Jul 2026). Local officials say Israel had never occupied some of those villages, and the deal lets the IDF stay in most of its security zone. "Handed ... to the Lebanese army" describes what happened without implying a wider withdrawal. The second half of the sentence is confirmed. As of 30 Sep–5 Oct 2026 there is still no US–Iran ceasefire, the counter-blockades continue, and Iran's president speaks of "renewed American attacks". It stays accurate.
- Sources: https://www.ynetnews.com/article/r1qycqo7me ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/21/lebanese-return-to-pilot-zone-villages-they-say-israel-never-occupied ; https://www.timesofisrael.com/iran-indicates-it-received-official-us-response-to-latest-offer-on-ending-war/ ; https://timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-5-2026

**R3. haredi-enlistment (More info)**
- Original: "Since then the army has sent tens of thousands of call-up orders, but only a few thousand have enlisted."
- Proposed: "Since then the army has sent tens of thousands of call-up orders, but only a small fraction have enlisted."
- Reason: the latest opened figure (TOI, May 2026) is more than 79,000 orders and only about 2,100 enlisted, which is roughly 2–3%. "A few thousand" slightly overstates that, depending on how it is counted. "A small fraction" is accurate and won't go out of date. Alternatively: "only about 2,000 have enlisted".
- Source: https://www.timesofisrael.com/haredi-parties-reject-latest-draft-exemption-bill-setting-course-for-sept-elections/
