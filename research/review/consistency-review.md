## Rules decided

One rule for each question the group reviewers raised. All are consistent with the Stage 4 brief; where a group had applied a different reading, the documents were changed as listed above.

1. **Contradiction needs a standing position.** `**Contradiction:**` is for an action that goes against the party's or leader's own stated position or pledge that was still standing when the action happened. If the position was stated only after the action, or the party had changed its position in public before it acted, the item is `**Change over time:**`.
2. **Ra'am in 2021–22, no Arab parties now.** Change over time for every party that sat in that government. It is a Contradiction only where a documented earlier pledge was broken, which applies to Bennett alone (May 2021 pledge, June 2021 government). yisrael-beytenu, yesh-atid, blue-and-white, the-reservists, yesodot-yisrael and byachad-bennett follow this; yashar-leyisrael states the fact without a label because its leader was not in politics then. new-hope follows it since its targeted re-run of 2026-10-12: no pledge about Ra'am from before June 2021 was found, and Sa'ar's own explanation of August 2026 is given.
3. **The 2022 enlistment bill.** Voting for the bill in January 2022 and against reviving it in June 2024 is Change over time for every party: the votes were cast in different Knessets, in coalition and then in opposition, either side of October 7, and each party's 2024 vote matched the position it held by then. Ministers who did not vote in 2022 are described the same way. Every 2021–22 coalition party's document now states both votes.
4. **Coalition-line votes and votes against a standing position.** A vote against a bill is a Contradiction where the bill matched a position the party held at the time, and only for votes cast after a dated position that the document cites: its election platform, a signed or published coalition agreement, or its own earlier vote. (Since the follow-ups of 2026-10-12 this wording and its application agree: the one agreement used as a dated position is cited from its signed text.) Votes cast before that dated position are split off as a Change over time. For a coalition-line vote the coalition's reason is given. Where no such position is documented, the vote is an Action, and the text says which platform was checked. On sovereignty the dated positions are: clause 118 of the Likud–Religious Zionist Party coalition agreement, signed and tabled in the Knesset on 28 December 2022 (likud-party, national-union-tkuma, atid-echad); the vote for the Jordan Valley bill on 22 January 2025 (jewish-national-front; Wasserlauf's vote for eretz-yisrael-shelanu), because Otzma Yehudit's own signed coalition agreement of 28 December 2022 has no sovereignty clause; the July 2025 sovereignty motion (shas, new-hope, agudat-yisrael, chomat-torat-yisrael). degel-hatorah never voted for a sovereignty measure and has no label. On the 2021–22 coalition votes the test is the party's platform for the March 2021 election, opened for every party but one: Contradiction for meretz (child allowances, disability allowance, public housing, transport fares for people with disabilities, law in Judea and Samaria), yesh-atid (disability allowance), yisrael-beytenu (public housing; death penalty from its own bills) and the-democrats-party (public housing); Action with "no matching pledge" for blue-and-white, new-hope, israel-first and byachad-bennett, and for the votes of the other parties that match no pledge; Action with "pledge already enacted" for yesh-atid's weapons-sentences vote; unlabelled for raam, whose 2021 platform could not be found, also after a second search in the follow-ups of 2026-10-12.
5. **Legal scope.** Listed: investigations opened, indictments, convictions, court rulings, Attorney General or State Attorney decisions, State Comptroller findings, Ethics Committee decisions, official disciplinary or administrative orders, Central Elections Committee rulings, and acts of foreign governments or international courts labelled as such. A detention without charges is listed with its status. Not listed: court cases a leader filed himself; civil settlements with no court finding; examinations in which a leader gave information and no finding was made; Knesset expulsion motions, except against a current candidate; complaints on which no official body acted. An official finding about a body the leader headed stays, with a status line saying it is not a finding about him personally (agudat-yisrael, blue-and-white, eretz-yisrael-shelanu). A finding about a party's accounts is listed for every party the report names. A decision by a minister or the prime minister about a person (an order barring someone from reserve duty, a suspension from cabinet meetings) is described in the body of the document and not listed as a legal item.
6. **Unnamed sources.** A report resting on unnamed sources may be mentioned only as the trigger for an on-record response, and no detail from it is stated as fact. Otherwise it is removed. A remark reported from a private conversation is not a pledge.
7. **Ynet questionnaire answers.** One tier: `**Statement (party's written answer):**`. Formal commitment is kept for platforms, coalition-agreement clauses and official party decisions, as Stage 2 defines it.
8. **Hamas and Hezbollah.** Glossary wording. No standalone or uncited framing sentence. An inline gloss at first mention is allowed: "Hamas (a terror organization)" and "Hezbollah (the Iran-backed terror organization in Lebanon)". Nothing is inserted into or after a quotation.
9. **"Expulsion".** Used only as an attributed quotation of a party's own word (zehut). It is not softened to "voluntary emigration", which would misstate that platform. No other document uses the word for a population in its own voice.
10. **Wikipedia.** Not cited where a better source exists. None of the 28 documents now cites it.
11. **Joint leaders' pledges.** A declaration signed by several party leaders is a Statement in every partner's document (8 September 2026 state-commission pledge; 26 September 2026 change-bloc declaration).
12. **Plans written before a split.** A plan co-authored before a split stays a Formal commitment in both documents, with the date of the split stated (the "Magen David" plan).
13. **Tallies.** The official record decides. A press figure is mentioned only where it differs. Where no per-MK record exists (the 6 July 2026 first reading), the text says the tally is the press figure.
14. **The July 2025 sovereignty motion.** Described by its operative clause (sovereignty over the areas of Jewish settlement). On its own it supports `partial-sovereignty-area-c`, not `full-sovereignty`.
15. **Recency of tier tags.** An item whose evidence is dated before 2021 carries `**Dated background (year; …):**` and not a live Action, Formal commitment or Statement tag, in every section.
16. **Voice.** Research notes are impersonal ("No … was found"), not first person.
